# ✈️ Airline Operations & Delay Analytics

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** for analyzing airline operations, flight delays, cancellations, diversions, airports, routes, and airline performance for 2024.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes airline flight operations to identify patterns in delays, cancellations, diversions, airline performance, airport activity, and route traffic.

The dashboard is designed to provide both high-level business insights and detailed operational analysis through interactive visuals, slicers, bookmarks, tooltips, and drill-through pages.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall airline flight operations.
- Identify patterns in arrival and departure delays.
- Compare airline performance.
- Analyze airport flight activity and delay intensity.
- Identify high-volume routes.
- Analyze flight cancellations and diversions.
- Provide interactive drill-through and detailed analysis.

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview
Provides a high-level summary of overall flight operations and key performance indicators.

### 2. Delay Analysis
Analyzes arrival and departure delay trends and overall delay performance.

### 3. Airline Performance
Compares airlines based on flight volume, delay rates, on-time performance, and operational performance.

### 4. Airport & Route Analysis
Analyzes airport activity, airport delay intensity, flight routes, and origin-destination traffic.

### 5. Cancellation & Diversion Analysis
Analyzes cancelled and diverted flights, cancellation rates, diversion rates, and their distribution across airlines and states.
